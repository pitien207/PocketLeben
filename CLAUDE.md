  screen games `sx-safe` (side notch). `src/fullscreen.ts`: Play → full screen + `screen.orientation.lock('landscape')`

  portrait phones to turn (once; `visa-hunter-rotate-hint`). New full-screen UI: check it at 740 × 340 too
